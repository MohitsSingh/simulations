📚 Key Theory: Distance, Modal Choice and Transport Cost

Different transport modes have different cost structures. Road has low fixed costs but high variable costs per km. Rail and Sea have high fixed costs (terminals, handling) but low variable costs, making them efficient for longer distances. The break-even distance is where two modes have equal total cost.

🚛 Road Transport: Advantages & Disadvantages

✅ Advantages

  • Low capital cost of vehicles
  • Flexibility of route choice
  • Door-to-door delivery capability
  • High speed for short-medium distances
  • Easy market entry for new operators

❌ Disadvantages

  • Traffic congestion in urban areas
  • Significant environmental impact
  • Road accidents and safety concerns
  • High maintenance costs for infrastructure
  • Fuel dependency and price volatility
