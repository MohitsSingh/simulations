🇬🇧 UK Smart Transport Initiatives

🚗 Automated Vehicles Act 2024

UK framework for AV safety and insurance. Self-driving vehicles expected on roads by 2026. £42bn industry by 2035.

📶 5G Testbeds Programme

Government-funded 5G corridors for connected transport trials on roads and railways.

🤖 Vivacity Labs AI

AI traffic sensors in Manchester reducing congestion and prioritising buses/cyclists.

🚌 CAV Trials

Autonomous shuttles in Cambridge, Greenwich. Self-driving bus in Fife on mixed traffic routes.
