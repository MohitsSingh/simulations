📚 Key Theory: The Triple Bottom Line (TBL)

Sustainable transport balances three pillars: Economic (affordability, efficiency, supporting growth), Social (accessibility, equity, safety, health), and Environmental (emissions, resource use, pollution). A truly sustainable system must perform well across all three dimensions.
