📚 Key Concept: Distribution Network Design

Distribution networks directly impact supply chain costs and customer satisfaction. The key trade-off is between cost (inventory, transportation, facilities) and responsiveness (response time, product availability). More facilities = faster delivery but higher costs. The optimal design depends on product characteristics and business strategy.

📖 Case Study: IKEA's Distribution Strategy

IKEA uses a hybrid approach: direct supplier-to-store for 50%+ of products, independent DCs for online orders, and in-store inventory for customer pickup. They minimise transport costs through flat-pack design and outsource last-mile delivery.
